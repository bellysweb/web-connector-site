YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

This page explains how information entered into the Web Connector enquiry form is handled.

Before launch: This is a starter privacy notice. Confirm it reflects your actual business practices, contact details, retention periods and legal obligations before publishing.

Enquiries

When you submit an enquiry, the form collects the details you provide, such as your name, email, phone number, business, service interest and message. The site forwards these details to the configured n8n webhook so Web Connector can respond. Configure n8n and any connected services to store and handle the information appropriately.

Retention and access

Set retention and deletion practices in your n8n workflow. Limit access to people who need it to respond to enquiries. Do not submit passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information through these forms.

Questions or removal requests

For a privacy question or to request correction or removal of submitted information, email hello@web-connector.win. Replace this address if you use a different business contact.
